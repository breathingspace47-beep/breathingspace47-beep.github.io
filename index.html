Who we are

Support that is personal, flexible and purposeful

Homage Inclusive Services is developing a local specialist service for children, young people and young adults with Special Educational Needs and Disabilities (SEND).

Our approach

We focus on bespoke, person-centred home and community support rather than relying solely on standard group-based activities. Our proposed model is designed to respond to individual needs, including complex needs, sensory vulnerabilities and the challenges associated with transition to adulthood.

Supporting families

Our service aims to provide meaningful and dependable respite for parent carers and unpaid family carers while helping young people build confidence, participate in their communities and develop pathways towards greater independence.

What we offer

Our proposed support services

Flexible support designed around the individual and their family.

⌂

Individualised Domiciliary Respite

Planned or flexible support within the family home, providing parent carers and unpaid family carers with a sustainable break from intensive caring responsibilities.

♡

1:1 and 2:1 Community Access

Supporting individuals to access local amenities, develop social confidence and enjoy recreational activities safely, with appropriate planning and risk management.

↗

Independence & Life Skills

Supporting young people and young adults with community orientation, practical life skills and pathways towards greater independence.

◷

Flexible & Out-of-Hours Support

Our proposed service model is designed to respond to high-demand periods, including early mornings, evenings, weekends and school holidays.

✦

Complex Needs Support

Tailored options for individuals whose behavioural or sensory needs may make traditional group environments difficult to navigate.

⇄

Transition to Adulthood

A focus on continuity, community participation and independence during the transition from children's services towards adult life.

Our focus

Supporting the 16–25 transition

Transitioning from children's services to adult social care can be an important period for young people and their families. Our proposed framework focuses on continuity and independence.
